# Moho — Roadmap

The roadmaps live in the wiki, one per line. Item status is not tracked in them; it lives on the
[project board](https://github.com/users/WrackedFella/projects/1). [`README.md`](README.md) indexes what exists.

| Line | Roadmap | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | [wiki/engine/roadmap.md](../wiki/engine/roadmap.md) | Milestones M0 to M5+ and their gates, engine waves, items out of scope, backlog, cross-line decisions |
| Flagstone (strategy) | [wiki/flagstone/roadmap.md](../wiki/flagstone/roadmap.md) | Strategy features in order, strategy decisions |
| Searchlight (FPS) | [wiki/searchlight/roadmap.md](../wiki/searchlight/roadmap.md) | FPS-M1 to FPS-M7, entry gates, exit goals, engine requests |

Update the roadmap of the line an item belongs to when a gate closes, an item moves between phases, or a decision is made.
